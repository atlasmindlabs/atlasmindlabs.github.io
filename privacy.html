Privacy Policy

Applies to AtlasMind Labs Mobile Applications

AtlasMind Labs respects your privacy. Our mobile applications are designed to operate with minimal data collection. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, our applications do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy applies to the following applications published by AtlasMind Labs:

  • Learn Spanish: EasySpeak
  • GRE Vocab Prep: Study Offline

Future applications published by AtlasMind Labs may also be covered by this Privacy Policy unless an app-specific Privacy Policy is provided.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. App data such as learning progress, unlocked content, preferences, settings, and other application data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to AtlasMind Labs.

Data Backup

Some of our applications may use Android's standard automatic backup feature to back up app data to your personal Google Drive account, if enabled on your device. Any such backup remains within your Google account. AtlasMind Labs has no access to this data. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Some of our applications also offer an optional manual backup feature that lets you export your app data (such as your learning progress) to a file. When you use this feature, you choose where the file is saved or shared using your device's standard share options. This backup file stays entirely under your control, and AtlasMind Labs never receives, stores, or has access to it.

In-App Purchases

Some AtlasMind Labs applications offer optional in-app purchases to unlock additional content or features. These purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. AtlasMind Labs never receives or stores your payment card or banking details. Payment information is handled by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Our applications contain no advertisements, use no advertising SDKs, and do not use or access your Advertising ID.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing (for optional in-app purchases)
  • Android Backup Service (if enabled by the user)

Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for users aged 13 and over and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our applications or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page along with the revised "Last Updated" date.
