Privacy Policy

EasySpeak

EasySpeak, developed by AtlasMind Labs, respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

Information We Collect

None. All app data — your progress, unlocked content, and preferences — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Data Backup

Your app data may be backed up to your own Google Drive via Android's standard backup feature. This data stays within your Google account; we have no access to it, and it is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

EasySpeak offers optional one-time purchases to unlock content. These are processed securely by Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment processing; we never receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Advertising

EasySpeak contains no ads, uses no advertising SDKs, and does not use an advertising ID.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases only)

Children's Privacy

EasySpeak is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
